Winner - Niral 2024 State Hackathon

Issued by Anna University · Sep 2024

The Naan Mudhalvan Niral Thiruvizha Hackathon is an initiative by the Tamil Nadu Skill Development Corporation (TNSDC) to foster innovation and entrepreneurship among final-year engineering students. Launched as a platform to address real-world challenges through technology-driven solutions, the hackathon attracted 8,486 idea submissions from 28,450 students across 290 institutions under 10 themes, including Agriculture, Health Tech, Smart Cities, and Renewable Energy. Following a rigorous multi-stage evaluation process by industry experts, investors, and startup incubators, the top 144 teams were shortlisted for the Grand Final Review, held across multiple cities in Tamil Nadu. Ultimately, 50 teams were selected for an Innovation Voucher Fund of ₹1,00,000 each, enabling them to transform their ideas into viable startups. I am proud to be one of the winners in this prestigious competition, having secured a place among the top 50 teams. The hackathon serves as a launchpad for young innovators, emphasizing impact, scalability, and commercialization potential in emerging technologies. LinkedIn

Best Paper Award - ICAME 2024

Issued by SRMIST · Mar 2024

Robotic manipulation of objects is a fundamental challenge in the field of robotics and automation. Accurate prediction of the number of fingers required to lift an object with a gripper is crucial for optimizing grasp planning and execution in various robotic applications. The proposed system is a novel approach utilizing computer vision techniques to predict the minimum number of fingers necessary to successfully grasp and lift an object of arbitrary shape and size. It employs a combination of RGB cameras to capture a 3D representation of the environment with technology like Augmented Reality and Deep Learning, including the target object. Through a series of image processing and machine learning algorithms, we extract key features such as object shape and size. These features are then fed into a Random Forest model based on Machine Learning, trained on a diverse dataset of objects with varying attributes. The trained model is capable of analyzing the visual input and estimating the optimal number of fingers required for a successful grasp, considering factors like stability and force distribution. This prediction helps the robot adapt its gripper configuration in real time, ensuring a more efficient and reliable manipulation of objects.

Runner Ups - ARMs Inventors Challenge 2022

Issued by STMicroelectronics, ARMs and AICTE · Dec 2022

Arm and All India Council for Technical Education have an academic partnership and signed a Memorandum of Understanding in August 2019. Together, enabling Engineering institutes to build capabilities for the semiconductor ecosystem through grants from Arm Education over the years. Arm Education and STMicroelectronics with support from AICTE’s Training and Learning Academy (ATAL) conceptualized and executed faculty workshops. As continuation of the workshops a contest “The Inventors Challenge-2022” was launched in the year 2022 resulting in Faculty members and student teams converting their ideas into a prototype. The Inventors Challenge reiterated the belief on the importance of the industry-academia knowledge sharing. The Inventors Challenge had 258 ideas submitted by faculty and student teams.
